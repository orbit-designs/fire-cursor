**Short description (1–2 sentences)**
An open-source WebGL cursor made of real, simulated fire. Flame trails, embers, smoke that dissolves the interface and liquid glass that catches the light. Free, zero dependencies, one line of code.

**Longer description**
Fire Cursor is a free, open-source experiment that turns the cursor into a living ring of fire. Nothing is pre-rendered: a heat field rises, swirls and cools unevenly, up to 120 times a second, so every flame is different. Moving fast leaves real flame trails and showers of embers. Interactive elements catch small flames on their edges, a fluid simulation dissolves words and cards into smoke along your path, and glass panes reflect the fire as the only light on the page. The showcase is a specimen sheet in five chapters plus a live playground that writes the code for you. On phones the fire follows your finger, and writes itself across the headline when nobody is touching the screen.
