Fire Cursor

A living ring of fire that follows your pointer — simulated in WebGL2, with flame trails, embers, edge flames and liquid-glass reflections. Move around and hover the cards and buttons.

View on GitHub

Flame trails

The heat is carried and cools behind the pointer, so moving leaves real trails.

Edge flames

Hover an element and its outline catches small flames near the cursor.

Liquid glass

Glass elements reflect the flames and embers, live, as the fire moves.
