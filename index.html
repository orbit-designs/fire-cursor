Play with fire.It follows you.

A living ring of flame that follows your pointer. Real simulated fire — trails, embers, smoke that eats the interface and glass that catches the light. One line of code.

Your device has reduced motion on, or no WebGL2 — the fire is resting. Everything else still works.

02 / 05Trails & embers

Move fast, leave a trail.

The heat is carried with you and cools behind you, so speed draws real flame trails. Embers break away — a few at rest, a shower when you rush.

03 / 05Edge flames

Edges catch.

Hover anything interactive and its outline catches small flames right where the cursor touches it. Embers rise from the edge, then everything cools when you move on.

04 / 05Smoke

Gone in smoke.

Sweep across a word or a card and it dissolves into real flowing smoke along your path — letters bend, fray and drift away. Stop, and it heals.

Glass catches light.

The fire is the only light in the room. Glass panes reflect the flames and every passing ember, and their edges glow on the side nearest the heat.

Three steps to fire.

      

        

          1
Download

          
Grab fire-cursor.js and, if you like, smoke-hover.js and glass.css from GitHub.

          Open the repository
        

        

          2
Add the scripts

          
Before the closing body tag:


<script src="fire-cursor.js"></script>
<script src="smoke-hover.js"></script>

          
        

        

          3
Light it

          
Start both — that's it. Add fc-glass to anything that should reflect the fire.


FireCursor.init();
SmokeHover.init();
